What we left out

A music player that just plays music.

Most music apps want something from you. Yoke doesn't. Here's everything it skips.

Ads

Not between songs, not in a banner, not anywhere. Your queue plays straight through.

Sign-up walls

Open the app and play. No email, no password, no "continue with" buttons.

Subscriptions

No monthly fee to listen to music you already have.

Tracking

Your library and listening history stay on your computer.

Locked features

Every feature is there from the first launch. Nothing sits behind an upgrade.

Rate-us pop-ups

No prompts, no trial countdowns, no "you might also like" interruptions.

New in Yoke

See it, hear it, make it yours.

The latest updates bring visualizers, 3D audio, your own background image, and a home for your live concert recordings.

Visualizers

Pick one of five visualizers to play with your music. Open it from the sidebar or press V, then go full screen.

  • Spectrum Bars · equalizer bars with falling peaks
  • Ribbons · glowing waveform lines
  • Halo · your album cover in a ring of light
  • Nebula · clouds of colour that bloom with the music
  • Warp · fly through the stars, faster on the bass

Live concerts

Add your recordings of live shows and they get their own Live section, grouped by show, each with its own album.

Cover art: fan-made Good Faith Forever live album cover, shared on r/madeon. See the cover on Reddit →

Your own background

Set any image as Yoke's background, like a photo, artwork or a favourite wallpaper.

3D

3D audio

Switch on 3D audio from the player bar for a wider, more spatial sound.
