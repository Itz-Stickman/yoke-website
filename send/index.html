    Help
    • Keep Yoke's From your phone screen open on your PC while sending, and keep this page open on your phone.
    • Your phone just needs internet. It doesn't have to be on the same Wi-Fi as your PC.
    • On Android, tap the ☰ menu in the file picker to browse Downloads, Music or another app's folder.
    • On iPhone, songs need to be in the Files app (for example, saved from another app with Share → Save to Files).
    • Songs downloaded inside streaming apps (Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube Music…) are locked to that app and can't be sent.
    • Songs aren't uploaded to this website. They travel encrypted from your phone to your PC.
